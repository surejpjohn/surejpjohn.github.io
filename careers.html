Get ready to apply

Show results on your CV

Start each point with an action verb and add a number where you can. “Grew Instagram followers by 30% in six months” says more than “Responsible for social media”.

Use STAR in interviews

Answer “Tell me about a time…” questions with the Situation, your Task, the Action you took and the Result.

Watch for job scams

Genuine employers never ask you to pay a fee to apply or to get a job. If an offer looks too good to be true, check with me or report it in the group.
